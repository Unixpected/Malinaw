    How this works

    1. The data is the DPWH Transparency Portal's project list, collected by csiiiv in the dpwh-transparency-data-api-scraper repository. The portal's own source is transparency.dpwh.gov.ph.
    2. Each rule uses only fields that exist in that data. The rules, their weights and their thresholds are listed below and live in rules.json, so anyone can change them.
    3. A project's score adds the weights of the rules it trips. An office is ranked by the budget-weighted average of its projects' scores. High and Medium for offices mean the top 5% and next 15% of ranked offices, so they are relative. For single projects, High is a score of 50 or more and Medium is 25 or more.
